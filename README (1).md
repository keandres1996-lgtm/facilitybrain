# FacilityBrain

> Digital Operations Center for Buildings, Facilities and Physical Operations.

## Vision
FacilityBrain is a SaaS platform that centralizes operational processes for organizations that manage physical spaces.

## MVP
- Authentication
- Organizations
- Sites
- Users & Roles
- Tickets
- Documents
- Operations Center

- shadcn/ui
- Supabase
- PostgreSQL
- Vercel
